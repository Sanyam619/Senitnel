# Overnight printer

`/app/kiosk/emit_card.sh` writes a draft tournament card by reading the
puzzle sheets and applying the desk's quick lane reading. That draft is a
convenience only. Several rounds in this booklet are traps that look ready
under the surface whisper; filing those drafts as wins fails the sealed
judge's road checks and the threat coverage rules.

When a finished card is already present at `/app/answers.json`, running the
printer again must leave the bytes unchanged.
