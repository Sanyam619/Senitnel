Complete an N-body Barnes-Hut campaign so its conserved observables satisfy documented numerical-agreement bands across checkpoint/resume and across domain-split worker counts. The campaign under /app advances bundled cases with a multipole treecode, mid-run checkpoints, and parallel domain decomposition; correctness means energy, total momentum, and mass closure stay within the bands in /app/docs/report-schema.md whether a case runs cold, resumes from a checkpoint, or is split across 1, 2, or 4 workers (force accuracy and reduction ordering must keep results worker-independent). Mid-run checkpoint materialization must also satisfy the "Checkpoint / padded-state halo" contract in that schema: after a round trip, ghost slots must carry the current interior-edge bodies rather than leftover pre-checkpoint ghost-buffer values, interiors must restore in live partition slot order, and the payload must cover interiors plus both halo strips. The verifier re-invokes that path with its own inputs, so a report that only looks numerically green is not enough if that contract fails.

Drive /app/scripts/run_campaign.sh over every case in /app/data/cases/ and write /output/campaign-report.json with schema_tag nbody-campaign-v1: one row per (case, workers, mode) carrying label, workers, mode, energy, momentum_L2, mass, and stable, plus a parity object with cold_resume_max_rel and worker_spread_max_rel. The verifier rebuilds /app/cmd/campaign from source and re-invokes internal modules with its own inputs, so reports must come from the real treecode binary. When run manifests under /app/config/manifests/ and compile-time defaults disagree on a physics knob, the manifest is authoritative for the observables you report.
