Operator notes
==============

Surface `/usr/local/bin/traefikhealth` reports process-facing status only. It
does not certify journal tip agreement, middleware prefer, or abort cutover.

Roster names and middleware names live under `/etc/traefik/roster.list` and
`/etc/traefik/mw.list`. Seating always re-enters `/app/ops/run_traefik_seat.sh`.
Hand-written ledgers under `/output/` are cleared on the next verify pass.
