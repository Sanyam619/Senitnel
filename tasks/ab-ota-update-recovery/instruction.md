Field units copied four raw flash images into `/opt/abdev/data/scenarios/` after mains dropped during over-the-air delivery. Symptoms vary by unit: sometimes the device boots an older userspace build, sometimes the controller refuses handoff citing integrity failure, and the redundant metadata copies at the front of each image disagree about which generation is current.

The on-disk layout is documented under `/opt/abdev/docs/flash_layout.md`. Canonical recovery precedence and finalize semantics are documented under `/opt/abdev/docs/recovery_policy.md`. The fleet rollout driver reads `/opt/abdev/config/active_policy.toml` and `/opt/abdev/config/recover.env` (which selects the policy file path), then invokes the prebuilt `/opt/abdev/bin/recover` through `/opt/abdev/ops/reconcile_field.sh`. Read-only diagnostics are `/opt/abdev/bin/slotstat` (boot-attempt counters only) and `/opt/abdev/bin/bootsim` (integrity check on a candidate image). The report schema is in `/opt/abdev/include/ab_report.h`.

Align the live rollout configuration with the canonical policy, run the field reconcile driver, and produce outputs for every bundled scenario under `/opt/abdev/data/scenarios/`: `/output/recovery.json` with `live_slot`, `action`, `bootable_slots`, and `bootloader_hex` per case, plus `/output/fixed_<basename>.img` for each input (`/output/fixed_case_alpha.img`, `/output/fixed_case_beta.img`, `/output/fixed_case_gamma.img`, `/output/fixed_case_delta.img`). Corrected images must stay the same byte length as their inputs, preserve payload and digest-chain sectors, and leave at least one integrity-clean live copy. Do not modify scenario images under `/opt/abdev/data/scenarios/` or rebuild shipped recovery binaries.
