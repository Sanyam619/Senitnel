# Desk notes

Evaluation preference and tip binding live under `/app/calib/`. While
selection stays in trial mode, or the tip bind receipt does not match the
registry-resolved durable tip, a rebuild restores seating surfaces from the
desk seed set. Source corrections in those surfaces do not survive a rebuild
until selection and tip binding are publishable together. The receipt is a
`key = value` sheet; a JSON body is not read.

AUUC and Qini must come from the scored column the estimator roster assigns
to the bound estimator, not from a raw observational mean-difference sweep.
Roster copies filed with the operator mirrors carry an older roster
generation. `/app/tools/upliftprobe` may print a stable-looking status line
during an unhealthy run.
