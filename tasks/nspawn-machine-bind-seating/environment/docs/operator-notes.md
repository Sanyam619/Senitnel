Operator notes
==============

not mean the seating ledger agrees with durable tip, bind attach, or
generation floors.

Prefer durable materials under /var/lib/machines/ over live sheets under
/etc/systemd/nspawn/ when the two disagree.

Frozen fixtures under /app/data/machines/ are packaging-pinned; seating must
not rewrite them.
