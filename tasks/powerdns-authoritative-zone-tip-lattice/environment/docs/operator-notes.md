Operator notes
==============

`/usr/local/bin/pdnshealth` probes config presence for the desk. A
serving print does not validate zone tips, preference mode, tip bind
acceptance, backing store retirements, generation floors, pdns.d fold,
record holds, or cutover receipts.

Durable apex data comes from `/app/data/pdns/` fixtures materialized
under `/var/lib/powerdns/zones/`. Roster order is
`/etc/powerdns/zone.roster`. Surface materials under
`/var/lib/powerdns/surface/` are used when preference is not durable.
