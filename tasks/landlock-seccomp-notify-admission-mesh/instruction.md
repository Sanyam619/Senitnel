Scenarios under /app/data/scenarios/ describe each job request (operation, notify wire hold or pass, descriptor epoch, claim, and a notify credential frame). Durable path seating is recovered from the sealed seating journal at /app/data/seating/canon.journal; the on-disk durable map under /app/data/roots/ may be incomplete after mesh churn. Incomplete on-disk durable seeds are not the tip to preserve across refresh. Rebuilds rematerialize surface seating knobs and notify integrity stubs until durable host preference is restored.

Host freshness for marked material uses claim numbers 4 through 9 inclusive. Marked claims inside that band refuse as epoch_revoke; marked claims outside the band are not refused for revoke on that alone. Descriptor epochs strictly behind the live runtime epoch quarantine as fd_stale; an equal descriptor epoch is current. An exec job with notify wire hold can quarantine as notify_skew with durable seating already clear. An open job with notify wire hold does not by itself force notify_skew. Durable identity outside the allow set quarantines as path_drift, and that result remains path_drift for an exec request that also carries notify wire hold.

Notify credential integrity uses signing material derived per frame from the fixture seed, the frame epoch low byte (rotated by an index-dependent amount), a small index-dependent stride, the frame lane id, and the weave strand from /app/ops/trust_policy.toml — not a plain seed copy. The integrity check is a keyed fold of each payload byte against that material (wrapping sum of payload XOR material). Surface plain-sum checks are not deep authority. Credentials signed only under a prior material schedule must quarantine as integrity_failure. Within each epoch-and-lane stream in capture order, timestamps must strictly advance; a non-advancing credential quarantines as replay with integrity otherwise valid. /app/bin/framecheck validates a single credential frame under those deep rules (prints ok on success); it is not a full admission publish path.
