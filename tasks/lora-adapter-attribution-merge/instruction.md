Four low-rank adapters live under /app/data/adapters/ as alpha, beta, gamma, and delta. Each was fit against a different base checkpoint under /app/data/bases/; the three bases are S1, S2, S3 in temporal order. Between S1 and S2 the tokenizer table was regenerated and the vocab grew; between S2 and S3 the residual-block normalizer was retuned (its eps dropped and the calibration energy recorded on the snapshot was revised). The five held-out downstream tasks the merge is scored against sit under /app/data/eval/ as task_1.json through task_5.json.

The inference stack now runs on S3 and consumes all four adapters together. The current pipeline stacks them naively: on the held-out evaluation the merged state regresses on some downstream tasks, the attribution accounting does not balance, and asking the pipeline to remove any single adapter's contribution from the merged state does not recover the score that a merge without that adapter would obtain. Ops has already committed to decommissioning gamma next quarter, so the single-adapter removal has to be reversible-through-attribution and not a full rerun.

The pipeline entry point is `/app/scripts/run_merge.sh`. It compiles the Java sources under /app/src/main/java and then invokes the merge driver to write `/output/merge-report.json`. Rebuild any time you touch source; the verifier compiles from scratch against your edits. The verifier also imports the pipeline packages directly with its own inputs, so replacing the driver with a hand-authored merge-report.json will not satisfy the checks.

The report carries a top-level schema_tag string and three sections named adapters, evaluation, and attribution. Field layout and numeric tolerances are documented in /app/docs/report-schema.md. Component expectations for how adapters are expressed against S3, how shares accumulate into the merge, and how single-source removal must behave are sketched in /app/docs/contracts.md — treat those as invariants to satisfy, not a patch recipe.

Do not modify anything under /app/data/ or /app/docs/.
